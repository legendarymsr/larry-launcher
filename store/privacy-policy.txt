Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 13.10.2026

Larry Launcher ("the App") is an Android home screen launcher with an optional Gentoo Linux environment, developed by legendarymsr ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us. We do not:
- Require an account or login.
- Use analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
- Show ads or use advertising identifiers.
- Collect device identifiers.
- Operate any servers that receive your data.

2. Local Data Storage

Your home screen layout, settings and, if you install it, the Gentoo environment and everything in it are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes the App's data. Removing Linux in the App's settings deletes the Gentoo environment, its files and its swap file.

3. Root Access

The Gentoo environment runs as a chroot on a Magisk-rooted device. The App asks Magisk for root only to set up, start, update and remove that chroot (mounting, SELinux policy for the chroot, and an optional swap file). Root is never used to read your other apps' data or send anything off the device.

4. Device Permissions

- Internet: used only for the downloads listed in section 5.
- Foreground service and notifications: keep the Linux environment running while it is in use and show its status and progress.
- Uninstalling apps: used only when you choose to uninstall an app from the app drawer. Android asks you to confirm every uninstall.
- Notification access (optional, off unless you grant it): lets the launcher show and control what is currently playing. Notifications are read on your device and are not stored or sent anywhere.

5. Network Connections

The App makes network requests only when you install or update the optional Gentoo environment. Each request reveals your IP address to the server, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

Gentoo (gentoo.org)
- The official Gentoo arm64 stage3 and the Portage tree, from distfiles.gentoo.org and Gentoo's mirrors.
- Packages from Gentoo's official arm64 binary package host (binhost), or, when no binary matches, source files from Gentoo's mirrors and upstream download sites.
- The optional desktop (niri) uses the GURU overlay, synced from Gentoo's infrastructure.
- Privacy Policy: https://www.gentoo.org/inside-gentoo/foundation/privacy.html

GitHub (github.com)
- Only if you enable the optional GPU driver on a supported Mali device, a pinned Vulkan driver file is downloaded from GitHub and checked against a fixed checksum.
- Privacy Policy: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Software you install inside Gentoo runs under your control and may make its own connections. We do not share any data with third parties ourselves.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly process data from children.

7. GDPR (European Users)

All data the App stores stays on your device and under your control. Network requests are made only to provide the features you choose to use.

8. Your Rights

You can:
- Revoke notification access and root access at any time in your device settings or Magisk.
- Remove Linux and its files in the App's settings.
- Delete all App data by uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.
